ℹ️ About

FreeAI is a free, open-source ChatGPT-style front-end. It talks directly from your browser to free AI providers — there is no FreeAI server, no account, no tracking, and your chats & keys never leave this browser. “Every model” = every model those free providers expose (the list is fetched live in the model picker), plus anything you plug in with your own free API key.
